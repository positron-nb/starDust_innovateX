# StarDust: event-day checklist

## Before the zonal (confirm officially and write down the answers)

- [ ] Team registered on techfest.org. Team ID noted and written on slide 1.
- [ ] Zonal centre, date, reporting time and slot confirmed by email or portal.
- [ ] Portal Doubt section checked: is an abstract upload required for Round 1? What is the word limit? (The brief contradicts itself on this.)
- [ ] Presentation format confirmed: laptop provided or own? HDMI? Clicker?
- [ ] Student IDs for all members.
- [ ] All placeholders on slide 1 filled in: name, ID, members, institute, contact.

## Files (keep three copies)

- [ ] PPTX and PDF on a laptop, on a USB drive, and in cloud storage or email.
- [ ] Test the PDF fallback on a second machine. If fonts are missing, present the PDF.
- [ ] A phone copy of the Q&A bank and the quick-revision sheet.

## Rehearsal

- [ ] 3 full timed run-throughs, each 9:00–9:20.
- [ ] 1 mock-jury round using the 15 hardest questions.
- [ ] Every speaker can explain h = z − d, Δh and why the system reports UNCERTAIN.
- [ ] Forbidden words drill: "results", "accuracy", "prevents floods", "world-first", "BMC partner".

## On the day

- [ ] Arrive early, load the deck, and check that slide 4 renders correctly (arrows and symbols).
- [ ] One person keeps time with signals at 5:00 and 8:00.
- [ ] Have appendix slides 8–12 ready for questions. Know their numbers.

## If something goes wrong

- **Projector or laptop fails:** present from the PDF on a phone or a printout, and talk through slide 4 on paper.
- **Running long:** drop the confounding examples on slide 5, then the metrics detail on slide 6.
- **Question you can't answer:** "We haven't verified that yet; it's on our test plan / site survey list."
